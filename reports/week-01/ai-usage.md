## Tools and usage

| Tool | What we used it for | Accepted | Changed | Rejected |
|------|---------------------|----------|---------|----------|
| GitHub Copilot | Drafting the Week 1 research structure: candidate list, alternatives, comparison table, gap analysis, value proposition, repository scaffolding. | Structural framing, property list, GAP/VP naming scheme. | All product claims were reviewed against the sources we actually looked at before being kept; competitor claims must still be verified by the team on the real sites. | Anything that asserted competitor facts we had not checked; generation-quality claims that are really assumptions (A3). |

## Accepted / changed / rejected detail

- **Accepted:** the overall structure of the research (problem-space sentence
  as the standard, properties fixed before evaluation, GAPs tested against
  four criteria, VPs closing GAPs with cost + competitor response).
- **Changed:** competitor specifics (e.g. CodinGame/LeetCode feature details,
  Rustlings mechanics) are only recorded as *observations to verify*; we did
  not let Copilot state them as verified facts without a source we
  checked.
- **Rejected:** Copilot producing *conclusions* about the market as
  established fact, and any fabricated URLs or metrics; those are flagged as
  assumptions (A1..A6) to test with the customer instead.
