# QR-04: Stay Available 99.9% Once Deployed

The product stays available for 99.9% of a week once deployed to the production host.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Reliability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Availability
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** High

## Scenario

### Source

A learner or instructor attempts to open the Debugging Gym in a browser during the week.

### Stimulus

The product is deployed on the production host and the learner requests the web app or a debug session.

### Environment

The product runs on the single VM of [`CON-01`](../../product-vision.md#con-01) alongside the `debug-runtime` containers, under the API-key budget of [`CON-02`](../../product-vision.md#con-02).

### Artifact

The `webapp` container serving the catalogue, generation, and verification, and the host that runs the `debug-runtime` containers.

### Response

The product shall serve the request and report the failure rather than hang when a runtime or the LLM provider is unavailable.

### Response Measure

The web app is reachable and serves a response in at least 99.9% of the minute-level checks in a week, measured from outside the host; a failure of a single `debug-runtime` does not take the whole product down.
The `Performance Efficiency` / `Time Behaviour` cost of a busy host stays below the response budget of [`QR-01`](QR-01.md) attach flow.
